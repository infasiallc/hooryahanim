How to Measure

Bust: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.
Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline.
Hips: measure around the fullest part of your hips.

Made-to-Measure

Every formal and bespoke piece can be crafted to your exact measurements at no extra cost. Simply share your measurements when ordering, or contact the house and we will guide you.

Measurements are a general guide. For heavily structured or embroidered pieces, please reach out before ordering.
