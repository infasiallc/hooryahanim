Delivery

Hand-finished in Islamabad, delivered to your door.

Every piece leaves our atelier wrapped with care. Here is how — and when — it reaches you.

Within Pakistan

Ready-to-wear pieces are dispatched within 3–5 working days. Orders within Islamabad & Rawalpindi are often delivered same-day or next-day. We ship nationwide through trusted couriers.

Delivery charges: Complimentary on orders above PKR 50,000. A flat PKR 500 applies to orders below this amount.

Made-to-Order & Bespoke

Hand-embroidered formal and bespoke commissions are created especially for you. These take 2–6 weeks depending on the intricacy of the work. Your estimated completion date is confirmed at the time of order.

International Delivery

We ship worldwide via international courier, typically arriving within 7–14 working days of dispatch. Please note that any customs duties or import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, we will share tracking details over WhatsApp or phone. For any question about your delivery, please contact the house or message us on WhatsApp.

Timelines and charges are indicative and may vary during peak wedding and Eid seasons.
