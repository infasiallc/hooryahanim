Care Instructions

Heirloom craft deserves gentle hands.

Hand-embroidery, zardozi, and fine silks are made to last generations — with a little care.

Embroidered & Formal Pieces

Always dry clean only. Zardozi, dabka, pearl, and sequin work should never be machine washed or soaked. Inform your dry cleaner that the piece is hand-embroidered.

Silk, Velvet & Organza

Dry clean only. Avoid spraying perfume or water directly on the fabric. Iron inside-out on a low setting, and never iron directly over embroidery — place a soft cloth between the iron and the work.

Lawn & Cotton (Everyday)

Gentle hand wash or delicate machine cycle in cold water with a mild detergent. Wash dark and bright colours separately for the first few washes. Dry in shade to preserve colour.

Storage

Store heavy formals flat or folded in a breathable muslin cloth, away from direct sunlight and damp. Avoid plastic for long-term storage. Let velvet rest folded to protect its pile.

When cared for gently, a Hooriya Hanım piece is meant to be passed down — from your wardrobe to your daughter's.
