Frequently Asked

Everything you may wish to ask the house.

A few answers to help you order with confidence. For anything else, simply write to us.

How do I place an order?

Browse the collection, choose your size, and add the piece to your atelier (cart). At checkout you can choose your payment method, or order directly with us over WhatsApp.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept Cash on Delivery, JazzCash / Easypaisa, and ordering via WhatsApp. Choose whichever is most convenient at checkout.

How long will my order take?

Ready-to-wear pieces are dispatched within 3–5 working days. Made-to-order and bespoke pieces take 2–6 weeks. See our delivery page for full details.

Do you offer custom or bespoke pieces?

Yes. We love creating bespoke commissions to your measurements and vision. Start a conversation on our bespoke enquiry form or over WhatsApp.

How do I know my size?

Please see our size guide. Every formal piece can also be made to your exact measurements at no extra cost.

Can I return or exchange a piece?

Unworn ready-to-wear may be returned or exchanged within 7 days. Bespoke pieces are final sale. Full details are on our returns page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 working days of dispatch. Customs duties are the recipient's responsibility.
